Help Center

How to Search for Movies

Use the search bar on the homepage to type the name of any movie. Press enter or click the search button to view results.

Viewing Movie Details

Click on any movie card or poster to open the movie details page, where you can see ratings, descriptions, release dates, and more.

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are connected to the internet.
  • Check that the movie title is spelled correctly.
  • Refresh the page if content is not loading properly.

Need More Help?

If you continue to experience issues, contact our support team at support@moviefinder.com
